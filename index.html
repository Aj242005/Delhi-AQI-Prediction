AV Anand Vihar Air Bulletin

        
One station, one honest line. Forecasts are built from hourly CPCB readings at DPCC Anand Vihar. Replay windows are recorded 2023 data and are always labelled; they are never today's air. Missing live hours are completed from CAMS reanalysis and kept separate from measured readings. Guidance is not medical advice.

        
Live data: DPCC via WAQI
Historical: CPCB via OpenCity
Weather and CAMS: Open-Meteo
Model: XGBoost · 2022 calibration · 2023 holdout
Times: IST (UTC+05:30)
