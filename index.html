ATMOSPHERIC & STATISTICAL GLOSSARY ('JARGON BUSTER')
Latest Hour vs. 24-Hour Average
CPCB's bulletin reports a 24-hour running average, while sites like aqi.in and WAQI show the latest hourly reading. The model was trained on hourly values, so this app feeds it the latest hourly sensor reading and shows an approximate 24-hour average (from the readings it has saved) for comparison only.
Precision vs. Recall on Severe Days
Recall measures the share of actual Severe (>400) hours caught (minimizing missed health warnings), while Precision measures how often a Severe alert is correct.
500 AQI Scale Ceiling
The Indian CPCB AQI scale tops out at 500, and 3,820 hours in our 2017–2023 dataset sit at that ceiling. Standard regression attenuates near 500, so we pair it with a calibrated severe-day classifier.
3-Way Chronological Validation
To prevent test leakage, hyperparameters and empirical prediction intervals are tuned strictly on 2022 validation data before evaluating once on the 2023 test set.
